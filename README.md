### ⚡ Design focus

With **nearly 10 years** in product design across **PropTech, e-commerce and branding platforms in Saudi Arabia and Europe**, I design products end to end and then build them. My work runs from research and design systems to working apps in the store.

- **Product design for B2B & SaaS:** complex workflows made calm, from rental management to admin consoles.
- **Arabic / RTL product design:** bilingual interfaces done properly, not mirrored as an afterthought. That means type pairing, numerals, plurals and layout logic.
- **Design systems:** tokens, component libraries, documentation and clean developer handoff.
- **Design → code:** I prototype and ship with modern tooling and AI-assisted development, so what I design actually ships.

🗣️ Arabic · English · Spanish
